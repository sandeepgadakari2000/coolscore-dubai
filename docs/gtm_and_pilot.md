# Go-to-market and 90-day pilot

## The offer (to one Dubai brokerage)

> "For 90 days we score every active rental listing you choose. Each gets a CoolScore badge with a cooling-cost
> range, a one-page unit report your agents can send, and an answer to 'how much is the AC?'. We measure what
> happens to enquiries, lead quality and time to let. Pilot fee **AED 15,000** (assumption; waivable in exchange
> for anonymised bill collection and a case study)."

## Who

A mid-sized brokerage (roughly 30–150 agents) with leasing listings in district-cooled tower communities.
Decision maker: head of leasing or the managing partner. Champion: a team leader who already gets the chiller
question.

## Timeline

| Weeks | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Pick 60–120 active listings (half get badges, half are matched controls by community, size and price). Agents trained (30 min). Baseline: last 90 days of enquiries per listing |
| 3–10 | Badges live in the brokerage's own channels (their listings pages, WhatsApp broadcasts, PDFs). Weekly check-in. Agents log "AC question asked?" per enquiry. Bill collection: agents ask landlords and tenants for anonymised figures |
| 11–12 | Analysis, trust survey (agents and a sample of enquirers), case study, go/no-go on a paid subscription |
| 13 | Decision: subscription (seats) or stop |

## Success metrics (decided before starting)

| Metric | How measured | Success |
|---|---|---|
| Enquiries per listing | Badge vs matched control, difference-in-differences vs baseline | +10 % or better |
| Lead quality | Share of enquiries reaching a viewing | No decline; ideally +5 pts |
| Time to let | Days from listing to signed lease | Not worse; ideally −5 % |
| Trust | 5-question survey: "the estimate was believable / useful" | ≥ 70 % agree |
| Data | Anonymised bill-months collected | ≥ 45 unit-months |
| Agent use | Unit reports sent per agent per week | ≥ 2 |

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Landlords object to a bad score | Opt-in by listing; frame as "true cost" plus concrete fixes; show ranges, never a bare "E" |
| Too few listings for statistics | Match controls; report effect sizes with intervals; treat as directional |
| Estimate is clearly off for a unit | "Report a correction" path: bill figures recalibrate; flag in the case study, don't hide it |
| Agents don't use it | Make it one tap (WhatsApp-ready report); weekly leaderboard; champion on the team |
| Legal (portals, data) | The brokerage's own channels only; no scraping; anonymised bills; PII guard |
