# Interview guide: brokers and leasing agents

**Who:** RERA-registered agents or team leads who do residential leasing or sales in Dubai (apartments).
**Length:** 20 minutes. Brokers are busy, so keep it tight. **Tests:** H4, H5, H6, plus resistance risk.
**Participant code:** B__

---

## 0. Opening and consent (1 min)

> "Thanks. I'm researching how running costs, especially cooling, come up when people rent or buy in Dubai. I'm not selling anything today. Your answers are anonymous, and I won't name you or your agency. OK to take notes?"

Consent to notes ☐

## 1. Context (2 min)

1. Which communities do you mostly work in? Leasing, sales or both?
2. Roughly how many viewings do you do in a typical week?

## 2. How cooling comes up today (7 min)

3. Think about your last few viewings. Did anyone ask about chiller, cooling or DEWA costs? What exactly did they ask?
4. How often does that question come up: every viewing, weekly, rarely?
5. What do you answer? Where does that answer come from? *(Landlord, building management, past tenants, a guide, a rule of thumb?)*
6. How confident are you in that answer? Has a client ever come back to say the bill was much higher than expected?
7. Do clients ask about **which direction** a unit faces or about the afternoon sun? Have you seen it affect a decision?
8. Does "chiller-free" change how fast a unit lets, or the rent it gets? Any example you can share (no addresses)?

## 3. Listings and differentiation (4 min)

9. When two similar units in the same building are listed, how do you make yours stand out?
10. What information do you wish you could put on a listing but can't easily get?

## 4. Concept reaction (4 min)

Describe it plainly:

> "Imagine a badge on a listing: 'CoolScore B · Est. cooling AED ▢▢▢–▢▢▢ per month', worked out from the unit's floor, facing, glass and cooling system, with a short explanation of why."

11. First reaction?
12. Would you want this on **your** listings? On all listings, including ones that score badly? *(Probe for resistance: "Would a landlord push back if their unit scored E?")*
13. Where would it help most: first enquiry, viewing, negotiation, or after signing?
14. Who decides whether a tool like this is used at your agency? Who pays for tools?
15. Would you try it on 5 of your current listings if I prepared the scores for you? *(This is the H6 commitment test. Write down the exact answer.)*

## 5. Close (1 min)

16. Do you know a landlord or tenant who might share anonymised cooling bills?
17. Who else should I talk to: another agent, a manager, someone at a developer?

---

## Watch for

- Words that signal resistance: "landlords won't like", "it'll scare people", "we don't want to be liable". Write them down word for word. They feed the hardest question in `interview_prep.md`.
- Agency tools and CRMs they already pay for (which tells us where we'd plug in). Record the tool type, not brand logos, in anything we publish.
