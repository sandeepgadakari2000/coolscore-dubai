# Interview guide: investors and landlords

**Who:** people who own one or more Dubai apartments they let out, or who bought within the last 3 years to rent out.
**Length:** 25 minutes. **Tests:** H7, plus chiller-free pricing and net yield.
**Participant code:** I__

---

## 0. Opening and consent (1 min)

> "Thanks. I'm researching how owners think about running costs, especially cooling, when they buy and let apartments in Dubai. Nothing is being sold. Your answers are anonymous, and I won't record unit or building names. OK to take notes?"

Consent to notes ☐

## 1. Portfolio context (3 min)

1. How many units do you own or manage? Which communities, roughly? (no building names)
2. Who pays cooling in your units today: tenant, you (chiller-free), or service charges?

## 2. Buying decisions (6 min)

3. Walk me through the last unit you bought. What numbers did you look at before buying?
4. How did you calculate yield? Gross, or net of service charges and other costs?
5. Did cooling (district cooling capacity charges, chiller-free status, orientation) enter that calculation? How?
6. Did facing or floor affect the price you were willing to pay? Why?

## 3. Letting decisions (8 min)

7. Have you ever offered a unit chiller-free? Why, or why not?
8. When you offer chiller-free, how do you decide how much more rent to ask? *(Probe for a method: rule of thumb, agent advice, last year's bills?)*
9. What did the cooling actually cost you in a chiller-free year? *(If they know, and only if they're comfortable, ask for typed figures later.)*
10. Have tenants ever complained or left because of cooling bills?
11. How long do your units sit empty between tenants? Does chiller-free change that?

## 4. Concept reaction (5 min)

> "Imagine a tool where you enter a unit's floor, facing, glass, size and cooling system. It shows the likely cooling cost range, your net yield after cooling and service charges, and the extra rent a chiller-free offer needs to earn to break even."

12. First reaction? Which of those numbers would you actually use?
13. When would you use it: before buying, before letting, or when renewing?
14. What would you need to see to trust it?
15. Would you pay for a report like this per unit? What do you compare it against? *(Don't name a price first.)*

## 5. Close (2 min)

16. Would you share anonymised cooling cost figures for one or more units? → `bill_request.md`. Consent to bill figures ☐
17. Anyone else I should talk to?
