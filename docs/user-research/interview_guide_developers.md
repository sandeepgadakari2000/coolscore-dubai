# Interview guide: developers

**Who:** product, design, sustainability, sales or marketing managers at Dubai residential developers or their consultants (MEP, facade, green-building).
**Length:** 30 minutes. **Tests:** H8, plus whether unit-level cooling cost influences design or sales.
**Participant code:** D__

---

## 0. Opening and consent (2 min)

> "Thank you. I have a civil engineering background, and I'm researching how unit-level running costs, cooling in particular, feed into residential design and sales in Dubai. I'm not pitching. I'd like to understand how decisions are made. Nothing you say will be attributed to you or your company. OK to take notes?"

Consent to notes ☐

## 1. Role and context (3 min)

1. What's your role, and which part of a project do you influence? (concept, design, approvals, sales, handover)
2. What kinds of projects are you working on now (mid-rise, high-rise, off-plan)?

## 2. Design decisions (10 min)

3. On a recent project, how were glazing ratio, glass specification and shading decided? Who had the final say?
4. Where do energy or cooling-load numbers come from during design? (MEP consultant, Al Sa'fat compliance model, rules of thumb)
5. Are those numbers ever broken down by unit, stack or facade, or only for the whole building?
6. When cost and aesthetics conflict (more glass versus lower cooling load), how is it resolved? Can you give an example?
7. How is the district cooling capacity per unit decided and communicated to buyers?

## 3. Sales and marketing (8 min)

8. Do buyers or brokers ask about cooling costs or chiller charges during sales? How do you answer?
9. Does sustainability or a green rating show up in your marketing? Does it move sales or price?
10. Are some stacks (e.g., west-facing) harder to sell or priced differently? How is that decided?
11. What do you hear from owners and tenants after handover about cooling bills?

## 4. Concept reaction (5 min)

> "Imagine a facade heatmap of a tower showing each unit's likely cooling cost (floors × directions), with sliders for glass ratio, glazing spec, shading and balcony depth that show the AED impact per unit and for the whole tower."

12. First reaction? At which stage would this be useful, if any?
13. Would a per-unit cooling-cost figure help sales (a selling point) or hurt them (a liability)?
14. Who would own a tool like this internally: design, sustainability, sales? How are tools like this usually bought?
15. What would it need to show for you to take it seriously? *(Accuracy versus a design-stage energy model? Alignment with Al Sa'fat?)*

## 5. Close (2 min)

16. Is there someone in design, MEP or sales I should also speak to?
17. Would you be open to a 15-minute look at a prototype in a few weeks? *(Commitment test for H8.)*
