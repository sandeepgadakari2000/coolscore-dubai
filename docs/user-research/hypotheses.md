# Hypotheses and decision rules

These thresholds were written **before** any interviews (Phase 0, 2026-10-03), so the results can't be bent to fit them later.
Record evidence as interview codes such as `T03` and `B02`. Leave the Result column blank until the evidence is in.

## Problem hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Evidence that counts | Pass threshold | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Cooling is a material, poorly predicted cost for Dubai tenants | Tenant describes a past bill that was higher than expected, or says cooling cost changed their budget | ≥ 4 of 10 tenants |  |
| H2 | Tenants can't estimate a specific unit's cooling cost before signing | Tenant used no tool and relied on the agent's word, a friend or an average | ≥ 6 of 10 tenants |  |
| H3 | A credible estimate would change which unit people pick | Tenant recalls a real trade-off (e.g., the cheaper rent was west-facing or not chiller-free) *or* ranks cooling cost in their top 3 decision factors | ≥ 3 of 10 tenants |  |
| H4 | Unit-level factors (facing, floor, glass) visibly change bills | Tenant or broker gives a concrete example of two similar units with different bills | ≥ 3 examples across all interviews |  |

## Channel and value hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Evidence that counts | Pass threshold | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H5 | Brokers are asked about cooling cost often and lack a confident answer | Broker says "weekly or more" *and* describes how they answer today | ≥ 3 of 5 brokers |  |
| H6 | Brokers would use a score in listings or conversations | Broker agrees to a concrete next step (try it on 5 listings, intro to a manager) — not "sounds nice" | ≥ 2 of 5 brokers |  |
| H7 | Investors price chiller-free offers and cooling on rules of thumb | Investor describes their current method; no unit-level model | ≥ 3 of 4 investors |  |
| H8 | Developers see design or marketing value in unit-level cooling cost | Developer names a real decision (glazing, shading, pricing per stack, sales story) where it would be used | ≥ 1 of 3 developers |  |

## Data hypothesis

| ID | Hypothesis | Evidence that counts | Pass threshold | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H9 | People will share anonymised bill figures | Typed figures for ≥ 3 months received | ≥ 30% of tenants asked |  |

## Decision rules (apply after ~10 tenants + 5 brokers)

- **Continue as planned:** H1, H2 and H5 pass, and either H3 or H6 passes.
- **Narrow to B2B (brokers/portals first):** H5 and H6 pass but H3 fails. Tenants accept the problem but wouldn't change their choice, so lead with listing differentiation rather than consumer decisions.
- **Narrow to investors:** H7 passes and tenant hypotheses are weak. Re-centre the product on net yield and chiller-free pricing.
- **Stop or rethink:** H1 *and* H2 fail. Cooling isn't a felt problem, or tenants already solve it, so write up the learning honestly. A well-reasoned "no" is still strong product evidence.
- **If H9 fails:** the real-world accuracy claim stays "not yet validated". Move bill collection to the broker pilot (brokers can ask landlords).

## Things that would surprise us (watch for these)

- Cooling bills are mostly the **capacity/demand charge**, so unit physics barely moves the total. That would weaken drivers such as "west-facing +AED X" and strengthen "ask about contracted capacity".
- Most tenants rent chiller-free, which would move the main value to landlords and investors.
- Brokers see a cooling score as a **risk** to their listings (resistance), not a tool.
