# Interview guide: tenants

**Who:** someone who signed a lease or moved within Dubai in the last 24 months, ideally in a tower apartment.
**Length:** 25 minutes. **Format:** call, video or coffee. **Tests:** H1, H2, H3, H4, H9.
**Participant code:** T__ (never write their name here)

---

## 0. Opening and consent (2 min)

> "Thanks for making time. I'm researching how people in Dubai work out what an apartment will really cost to live in. I'm not selling anything, and there are no right answers. It's your experience I'm after.
> Your answers are anonymous: I write down a code, not your name. Is it OK if I take notes? [If recording:] Is it OK if I record audio, just for my own notes? It'll be deleted after I write up."

Note: consent to notes ☐ consent to recording ☐

## 1. Warm-up (2 min)

1. How long have you lived in Dubai? Which area do you live in now?
2. Do you live alone or with others? (count only, no names)

## 2. The last move: tell the story (8 min)

3. Take me back to the last time you looked for a place. What were you looking for?
4. How many places did you seriously consider? What made you pick the one you chose?
5. When you compared places, what costs did you add up on top of rent? *(Listen for: DEWA, chiller, service, Ejari, agency fee. Don't prompt cooling yet.)*
6. *(If cooling wasn't mentioned)* Did air-conditioning or chiller costs come up at all while you were deciding? How?
7. Before you signed, did you know whether the building was chiller-free, district cooling or DEWA-billed AC? How did you find out?
8. Did you try to estimate the cooling cost before signing? **How exactly?** (Asked the agent, asked a friend, used a calculator or guide, guessed?)
9. What did the agent say about cooling cost? How confident were they?

## 3. Reality after moving in (6 min)

10. What was your first summer cooling bill roughly, in AED? How did that compare to what you expected?
11. Which months are worst and best? Roughly how much in each?
12. Has anything about the bill surprised or annoyed you? *(Listen for: capacity/demand charge, deposits, fuel surcharge, the bill not falling in winter.)*
13. Which direction does your living room face? Which floor are you on? Is there a lot of glass?
14. Do you know anyone in a similar flat with a very different bill? What do you think explains the difference?
15. What temperature do you usually keep the AC at? Is someone home during the day?

## 4. Value and priority (3 min)

16. If you'd known the likely cooling cost for each place you looked at, would anything have changed? *(Probe for a specific unit and a specific reason; "maybe" isn't evidence.)*
17. Rank these in how much they mattered in your choice: rent, location/commute, size, view, building quality, total monthly running costs.

## 5. Concept reaction (3 min, last)

Show or describe the concept card below. **Don't** sell it. Read it out and stop talking.

> **Fictional listing, illustrative only:** 1-bedroom, Business Bay (fictional tower), 14th of 30 floors, west-facing, floor-to-ceiling glass, district cooling, tenant pays.
> **CoolScore D · Est. cooling AED ▢▢▢–▢▢▢ per month (summer), AED ▢,▢▢▢–▢,▢▢▢ per year.**
> True monthly cost = rent + cooling. Biggest driver: west-facing glass.
> *(The numbers are blank on purpose: the model isn't built yet. We're testing the idea, not the figure.)*

18. What's your first reaction? What would you do with this?
19. What would make you trust or distrust this number?
20. Where would you want to see it: on the listing, in a separate app, or from the agent?

## 6. Close (1 min)

21. "Would you be willing to share a few months of your cooling bill figures, typed out with no names or account numbers? It's the only way to check whether the estimates are right." → If yes, send `bill_request.md`. Consent to bill figures ☐
22. "Is there anyone else who recently moved and would talk to me for 20 minutes?"
23. Thank them. Offer to share the results when the tool is ready.

---

## Interviewer notes

- Silence is fine. Wait three seconds before your next question.
- Follow the story, not the script. Skip questions they've already answered.
- If they ask what you think, deflect: "I'm still learning. What's your take?"
- Write down exact words for anything surprising, and mark paraphrase with `~`.
