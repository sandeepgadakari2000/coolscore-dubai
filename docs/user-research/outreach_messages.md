# Outreach messages

Replace everything in `[brackets]`. Keep messages short and personal, and send one follow-up at most.
Be honest about what this is: a product research project, not a company and not a sales pitch.

LinkedIn connection notes are limited to about 300 characters (less on some free accounts), so the **connection note** versions below are kept short.

---

## Tenants

**WhatsApp (friends, friends of friends)**
> Hi [Name]! Quick favour: I'm researching how people in Dubai work out what an apartment really costs to live in (rent + DEWA + cooling). Did you move in the last couple of years? I'd love 20 minutes of your story, by call or over coffee. No selling, and it's anonymous. Would [day] or [day] work?

**WhatsApp (community or expat groups — only where the group rules allow research posts; ask the admin first)**
> Hi all, I'm Sandeep, a civil engineer and MBA doing independent research on apartment cooling costs in Dubai (chiller, district cooling, DEWA AC). If you've rented in the last 2 years and could spare 20 minutes for an anonymous chat about how you chose your flat, please DM me. Thank you!

**Follow-up (once, after 3–4 days)**
> Hi [Name], just floating this back up in case it got buried. Even 15 minutes would really help. No worries at all if not!

## Referral ask (end of any interview)

> Thank you, this was really helpful. Is there one person you know who recently moved, or who works in leasing, who might talk to me for 15 minutes? A WhatsApp intro would be amazing.

## Do / don't

- **Do** personalise the first line (their community, a post they wrote, a mutual contact).
- **Do** offer two specific time slots.
- **Don't** attach decks or links in the first message.
- **Don't** call it a startup or claim partners you don't have.
- **Don't** message the same person more than twice.
