How is this calculated?

True cost per month = annual rent ÷ 12 + typical (P50) simulated cooling ÷ 12 + Dubai Municipality housing fee (5% of rent ÷ 12). Cooling ranges are P10–P90. Other electricity, water and service charges are not included.
