12-month P&L table
How is this calculated?

Revenue = pilot fee (month 1) + broker seats × price (from month 4, with churn) + scored portal listings × price (from the portal start month) + developer reports (quarterly) + consumer reports. Costs = AI parsing per listing (Claude Haiku 4.5 list price × tokens) + fixed monthly costs + part-time sales from month 4 + founder salary (0 by default: unpaid in year 1).
