Why a portal might show it

It answers the most common unasked question (“what will the AC cost?”), lets buyers filter by true cost, and gives good units a reason to stand out. Low-scoring units get a concrete fix (blinds, film, AC temperature) instead of a vague reputation.

Rules for a fair badge

  • Show the range, not a single number.
  • Label it as simulated — on the badge itself.
  • Let owners add real bills to calibrate it.
  • Score every listing, not only the good ones.
